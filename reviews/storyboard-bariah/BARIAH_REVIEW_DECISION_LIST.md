## `D-01` — Scenario and cast · **S02**

**What is proposed.** One bounded site-context dialogue, five turns, between two **role-neutral**
characters — `[PELATIH]` and `[PENYELIA TAPAK]`. The exchange establishes that landscape components
split into two groups with different functions and installation methods, which is the premise the
whole bahagian rests on.

The dialogue is on screen S02 in full. Its content is derived from module pp. 237 and 242; **the
dialogue lines themselves are written, not transcribed** — the module contains no dialogue.

**Why role-neutral.** The current B02 cast cannot be established from any artifact held. `Haziq` and
`Encik Roslan` were superseded; `Alya` and `Encik Rahman` appear **nowhere** in any package that was
searched; the course-wide Cast Bible is `OPEN`. The casting prompt that was found reads
`watak (Haziq/Roslan/baru)` — `baru` means the bank was a menu, not a closed cast. Naming anyone would
be an invention.

**What Bariah decides**

1. Is the scenario premise right — two workers on a planted site, discussing what remains to be built?
2. Five turns, or shorter?
3. Named cast or role-neutral? If named — **which names**, and does that decision bind PL06 or only B02?
4. Does the learner click through each turn, or does the whole exchange play as one VO run?

**If named cast is approved,** every role token in the deck is replaced in one pass. That is a
mechanical change, not a rebuild.

## `D-08` — Quiz · **S18**

**Five items are drafted: four MCQ and one Multiple Response.** Every item is derived from a named
module page. Each carries its options, its correct answer and both immediate-feedback branches.
Stems and answer keys are on the screen; the complete item bank is in the S18 notes page.

**The module contains no question bank.** These items are **constructed from source content**, not
transcribed. They are marked `DRAFT_FOR_BARIAH_REVIEW` on the screen, in the notes and in the file
properties. **They are not approved assessment content.**

### Soalan 1 — MCQ · module p. 238 (3.3.1)

> Struktur persisir air dibina di tepi badan air seperti tasik, sungai atau pantai. Apakah keperluan
> pembinaan yang paling kritikal bagi struktur ini?

- A. Warna permukaan yang menarik
- **B. Asas yang stabil dan rata dengan saliran permukaan yang berkesan** ← correct
- C. Ketinggian struktur melebihi dua meter
- D. Penggunaan bahan import

*Betul:* Persekitaran lembap dan berpotensi hakisan memerlukan asas yang stabil dan rata, serta saliran
permukaan yang berkesan.
*Salah:* Rujuk semula Struktur Persisir Air — asas yang stabil dan saliran permukaan yang berkesan
adalah keperluan kritikal.

### Soalan 2 — MCQ · module p. 239 (3.3.2)

> Manakah antara berikut merupakan struktur teduhan tradisional Melayu yang digunakan sebagai tempat
> berehat?

- A. Pergola · B. Canopy · **C. Wakaf** ← correct · D. Gazebo

*Betul:* Wakaf ialah struktur tradisional Melayu yang digunakan sebagai tempat berehat.
*Salah:* Pergola ialah rangka struktur terbuka yang ditumbuhi tanaman pemanjat. Wakaf ialah struktur
tradisional Melayu.

### Soalan 3 — MCQ · module p. 240 (3.3.3)

> Apakah pertimbangan utama dalam pemilihan bahan bagi kemudahan awam seperti tandas dan surau di taman?

- **A. Bahan yang tahan vandalisme dan cuaca** ← correct
- B. Bahan yang paling murah di pasaran
- C. Bahan yang perlu dicat semula setiap bulan
- D. Bahan yang seragam dengan perabot taman

*Betul:* Kemudahan awam memerlukan bahan yang tahan vandalisme dan cuaca, serta mudah diselenggara.
*Salah:* Rujuk semula Kemudahan Awam — ketahanan terhadap vandalisme dan cuaca adalah pertimbangan utama.

### Soalan 4 — MCQ · module p. 241 (3.3.4)

> Dalam pembinaan water feature, apakah aspek kawalan kualiti air yang dinyatakan dalam modul?

- A. Penambahan garam · **B. Pencegahan alga** ← correct · C. Pemanasan air · D. Pewarnaan air

*Betul:* Kawalan kualiti air termasuk pencegahan alga.
*Salah:* Pencegahan alga adalah sebahagian daripada kawalan kualiti air bagi water feature.

### Soalan 5 — Multiple Response · module pp. 242–249 (3.4.1–3.4.3)

> Pilih SEMUA komponen yang tergolong dalam Perabot Taman.

- **A. Kerusi Taman** ✓ · **B. Papan Tanda** ✓ · C. Boardwalk · **D. Tong Sampah** ✓ ·
  **E. Drinking Fountain** ✓ · **F. BBQ pit** ✓ · G. Gazebo

Correct: **A, B, D, E, F**

*Betul:* Perabot taman merangkumi Kerusi Taman, Papan Tanda, Tong Sampah, Drinking Fountain dan BBQ pit.
*Salah:* Boardwalk ialah struktur persisir air dan Gazebo ialah struktur teduhan — kedua-duanya struktur
taman, bukan perabot taman.

**What Bariah decides**

1. Are all five items factually correct against the module, and are the answer keys right?
2. Is one Multiple Response the right mix, or should there be more than one?
3. Are the distractors fair — particularly Soalan 5, where `Boardwalk` and `Gazebo` are drawn from the
   *other* group deliberately?
4. Is feedback the right register — corrective and pointing back to the screen, or shorter?
5. Five items for one bahagian — right length?

## `D-12` — Title-bar italic on lexicon terms — **deviation, needs a decision** · **S08 S14 S15**

The rule is that the three lexicon terms carry italic **wherever they appear in display**. The accepted
visual sample v0.2 applies it to card labels and body text but **leaves the 40 pt title bar roman** on
the three screens whose title *is* a lexicon term.

**This storyboard italicises the title bar**, on the reading that the title bar is display text and the
rule says *wherever*. That is a **deliberate deviation from the accepted sample** and it is called out
here rather than absorbed silently.

**What Bariah decides.** Italic title bar — as built here — or roman, matching the accepted sample? If
roman, the rule should be restated as *"wherever they appear in body text and labels"*, so it stops
reading as a violation.
